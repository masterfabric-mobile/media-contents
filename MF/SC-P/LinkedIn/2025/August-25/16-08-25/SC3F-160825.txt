#MFSC3F - 16.08.25 | Define the data strategy FIRST. Then build with clarity.

Without the right strategy, data becomes noise.
The Data-First Approach helps teams stay aligned, reduce waste, and deliver with precision.

Here’s why it works:

🔷 Goals Before Gathering
Start by asking the right questions. Clear goals prevent irrelevant collection.

🔹 Source Mapping Before Modeling
Trace where data comes from before building schemas — reduce duplication and confusion.

🔹 Ownership from the Start
Assign clear responsibility for every dataset. Governance begins early.

🔹 Aligned Metrics and Definitions
Shared language ensures consistency, trust, and clarity across teams.

🔹 Documentation as a First-Class Asset
Data without context is noise. Good docs = better onboarding, adoption, and accuracy.

Great data starts with great intent. Strategy comes first—everything else builds on top.
