#SCP-23-08-25 | Why Abstraction Defines Good Architecture

🧩 Abstraction isn’t about hiding complexity — it’s about managing it with intent.

In modern software, clear boundaries enable flexible change. When each layer knows just enough, we reduce coupling, improve testability, and create systems that don’t fall apart under pressure.

This is how good architecture survives growth:
— Interfaces stay stable
— Logic remains independent
— Teams move faster, with confidence

In OSMEA, abstraction is not a refactor—it’s a foundation.

🔍 We design for what matters:
– Maintainability over quick hacks
– Stability over overexposure
– Strategy over shortcuts

💬 Architecture is not just structure. It’s protection, clarity, and long-term thinking.
