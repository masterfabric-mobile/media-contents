#SCP-14-12-25 | Scaling Without Rewriting Everything

Scaling isn’t about starting over.
It’s about designing systems that grow without breaking.

Most teams rewrite when things get slow, fragile, or messy. But the real problem?
🧩 Tight coupling
🧩 Buried logic
🧩 No clear boundaries

Sustainable scale comes from:
✅ Decoupling intent from implementation
✅ Designing with extension in mind
✅ Protecting what already works

Good architecture doesn’t fear growth — it invites it.
