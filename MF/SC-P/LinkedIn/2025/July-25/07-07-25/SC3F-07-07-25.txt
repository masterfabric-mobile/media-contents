Here’s the 3F API Approach — a practical mindset for scaling software with clarity, speed, and resilience:

🔵 API-First
Define early, align fast. A shared API contract helps teams build in parallel without miscommunication.
Design first, build better.

🟠 API-Fast
Use mock APIs to decouple frontend and backend efforts. Accelerate testing, feedback, and delivery.
Mock early, move faster.

🟢 API-Flex
Plan for change from day one. Versioning and compatibility ensure APIs can evolve without disruption.
Evolve without breaking things.

From alignment to acceleration to adaptability — 3F is your API superpower.
