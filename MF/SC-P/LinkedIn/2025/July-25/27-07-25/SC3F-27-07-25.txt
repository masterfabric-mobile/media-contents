#MFSC3F - 27.07.25 | Define the experience FIRST. 

Then shape the interface. Great products don’t start with screens. They start with understanding people.

The UI/UX-First Approach ensures user needs guide every decision — from flows to features to final polish.

Here’s why it works:

🔹 User Needs Before UI Decisions
Solve real problems, not just create pretty screens.

🔹 Flows Before Frames
Map journeys early to ensure structure matches the experience.

🔹 Cross-Team Alignment from the Start
Design, product, and dev teams unite around shared intent.

🔹 Prototype Early, Test Rapidly
Validate direction fast, uncover blind spots, lower risk.

🔹 Design That Guides Development
Smooth handoff, fewer redesigns, and better outcomes.

Experience-first design leads to products people love — and teams ship faster with confidence.
