#SC-3F-301025 – 🔍 RESEARCH – 3F SUMMARY

Research isn’t a report—it’s a rhythm.
With Research-First, Research-Fast, and Research-Flex, learning becomes part of how teams build, not a separate step.

🔵 Research-First
Start with clear intent. Align teams early. Build what matters.

🟠 Research-Fast
Test assumptions quickly. Get rapid feedback. Avoid costly detours.

🟢 Research-Flex
Adapt to change, scale across contexts, and keep learning relevant.

Whether you’re validating an idea or adjusting mid-sprint, 3F helps you research with purpose—and move with confidence.
