#SC-CL-111025 - 🍒 Git Cherry-Pick

Where commits travel. When context stays.

Cherry-pick isn’t merging — it’s precision transplanting.
Use it to bring a specific commit into another branch without the full branch history. Clean, targeted, and powerful — if used with care.

In Code Lexury, cherry-pick is for those who move with intention — not by accident.

🔍 Learn:
– When to cherry-pick instead of merge or rebase
– Where conflicts commonly arise
– What it means for commit history and traceability
