SC-3F-201025 – QUALITY-FIRST

🧪 Quality is not a task — it’s a mindset.

Good teams test what they build.
Great teams build with testability in mind from the start.

Why it works:

Test strategy before code prevents guesswork later.

Shared “Done” definition eliminates ambiguity.

Early QA involvement catches issues before they become blockers.

Well-defined requirements = better coverage.

Quality culture is built, not bolted on.

📌 Define quality first. Then ship with confidence.
