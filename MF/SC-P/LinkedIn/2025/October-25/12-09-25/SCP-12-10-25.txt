#SCP-12-10-25 | State Is Hard — Here’s Why

Managing state isn't just about storing values.
It’s about tracking what happened, what changed, and what that change should mean — across UI, logic, and time.

So why does state break so many systems?
🔹 It lives in too many places
🔹 It changes silently
🔹 It drives what users see — directly

But with the right mindset:
✅ You centralize what matters
✅ Make change predictable
✅ Avoid hidden bugs before they scale

State isn’t just a dev concern — it’s a product risk.
Great teams know: no system stays stable without it.
