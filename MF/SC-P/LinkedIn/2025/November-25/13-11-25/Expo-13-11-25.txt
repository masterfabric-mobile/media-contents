At MasterFabric, we focus on equipping developers with modern, efficient, and scalable tools — and for mobile development, that means Expo.

📱 Why Expo?
We selected Expo as our designated cross-platform framework to help participants build end-to-end mobile solutions with clarity and speed. It removes unnecessary complexity, enabling teams to move from prototype to production environments without friction.

🔗 Unified React Ecosystem
Our program teaches React for the web and extends the same ecosystem to mobile through React Native + Expo.
This allows participants to apply their React knowledge directly to mobile development, ensuring architectural consistency across platforms — a core pillar of the “architectural thinking” approach we cultivate.

With Expo, teams experience a streamlined, AI-supported workflow where web and mobile align under one coherent development philosophy.
