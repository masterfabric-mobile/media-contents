#SC3F-201125 – 🧪 TESTING 3F

Testing isn’t just a step — it’s a mindset.
The 3F model helps teams test smarter, move faster, and adapt confidently.

🔵 Testing-First
Plan before you build. Define coverage and ownership early.
Start with testing — then build with confidence.

🟠 Testing-Fast
Automate, parallelize, and get fast feedback.
Test fast. Fix faster. Ship with trust.

🟢 Testing-Flex
Adapt testing as systems evolve. Avoid rigid workflows.
Keep testing flexible — and always in sync.

🔁 Shift left. Then move right.
