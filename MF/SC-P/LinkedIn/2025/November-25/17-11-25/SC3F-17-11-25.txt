#SC-3F-171125 – ✅ TESTING-FIRST

Testing is not a phase — it’s a mindset.
Testing-First means testing before the code is written, not after.

Why it works:

🧠 Quality Starts With Planning
Define test strategy and goals before you build — not after things break.

🧾 Acceptance Criteria Drive Clarity
If it’s not testable, it’s not done.

🧱 Test Pyramid by Design
Balance unit, integration, and E2E from the start. Focus where it matters.

🤝 Shared Ownership From Day One
QA, dev, and PMs align early on what and how to test — together.

🐞 Fewer Bugs, Fewer Surprises
Catch issues early. Boost confidence. Ship faster.
