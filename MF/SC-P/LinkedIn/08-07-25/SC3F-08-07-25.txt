🧱 Why build before you’ve mapped the system?
The Architecture-First approach flips the script: Instead of diving into code, you design structure, boundaries, and responsibilities first. Here's why that’s a game changer:

🔷 Systems Designed Before Code Is Written
A clear blueprint helps teams align, scale, and ship with confidence.

🔹 Clear Structure, Clean Dependencies
Early planning prevents tangled code, undefined ownership, and technical debt.

🔹 Better Team Alignment from Day One
Everyone—from devs to product to DevOps—works from a shared understanding.

🔹 Prevents Costly Refactors
Avoid painful rewrites and scalability failures by getting it right up front.

🔹 Enables Domain-Driven Design
Modular thinking and bounded contexts thrive when architecture leads.

💡 Don’t start with code. Start with clarity.
Design the architecture FIRST. Then build with confidence.
